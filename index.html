Her story

Four very different rooms, one way of showing up

Nadia has built a career across technology, media, business and community work. She is at home in a boardroom, a ballroom and a community hall.

  • The corporate floor

    Nadia worked in IT at Vodacom, one of South Africa's largest companies. She knows deadlines, systems and what it takes to be heard in a technical team.

  • The camera

    Her modelling work taught her presence, timing and how to stay composed when every eye is on her. It is the craft behind how she carries a stage as an MC.

  • The family home

    She founded Smartcare 911, a Cape Town company that provides at-home care so older people can keep living safely and independently.

    The company began while her late mother, Chanbebe, was unwell, and it still runs on her mother's words: “Care for others as you would care for me.”

  • The training room

    Through upskilling initiatives she has helped more than 2,000 women gain the information and skills to earn a sustainable income. She believes in dignified work over handouts.
